Our History

East Toronto Community Legal Services was founded in the early 1970s by Jim Renwick. He started a drop-in legal service at Woodgreen Community Centre, which eventually became Riverdale Socio-Legal Services.

In 1984, we changed our name to East Toronto Community Legal Services to better reflect our expanded service area. Today, we are one of over 80 community legal clinics funded by Legal Aid Ontario.

Who We Are

Our Staff

Our office is staffed by experienced lawyers and paralegals who understand the legal challenges facing low-income families in East Toronto. We are also supported by students, volunteers, and articling students.

Our Board of Directors

Our Board of Directors sets policies and oversees the clinic's work. Board members are elected each year from our membership and represent our community.

We need board members! If you live or work in our catchment area and want to volunteer 5-6 hours each month, we would love to have you. Board members help make important decisions that shape how we serve the community.

People of all backgrounds are welcome — especially those with lived experience of legal problems. Learn more about becoming a board member.

What We Do

East Toronto Community Legal Services is more than just a law office. We are a legal resource centre for our community. We do three kinds of work:

1. Help Individual People

We provide free legal advice and representation to low-income residents on a case-by-case basis. Whether you need help with benefits, housing, employment, or other legal issues, we are here for you.

2. Educate the Community

We run free public legal workshops and seminars on topics that matter to people in our neighbourhood. We also publish legal information in many languages so people can understand their rights.

3. Fight for Systemic Change

We take on test cases and law reform work to change laws and improve legal rights for everyone in our community. We also help community groups and tenant organizations get stronger and have a voice.

Our Values & Mission

We believe that:

  • Everyone deserves access to legal help, regardless of income
  • Legal help should be provided with respect and a human touch
  • We should work to meet the diverse needs of our multicultural community
  • We should partner with other community agencies to help people access all the services they need
  • Community members should have a say in what legal issues matter most
  • We should work toward changing laws and systems that harm low-income people

Funded by Legal Aid Ontario

East Toronto Community Legal Services is funded by Legal Aid Ontario (LAO). LAO is the Crown corporation that provides legal aid in Ontario — helping people who cannot afford a lawyer.

To learn more about legal aid in Ontario, visit Legal Aid Ontario's website.

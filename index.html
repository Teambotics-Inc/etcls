Welcome

East Toronto Community Legal Services provides free legal advice and help to low-income residents living in our neighbourhood.

Funded by: Legal Aid Ontario

Our Catchment Area

We serve people living in the area bounded by:

  • Don River (west)
  • Victoria Park Ave. (east)
  • Danforth Ave. (north)
  • Lake Ontario (south)

What We Can Help With

We provide legal advice and representation in many areas of law. See our Services page for details on what we offer.

More Than Just Legal Advice

Free Legal Workshops

We run free public legal education seminars and workshops on topics that matter to people in our neighbourhood.

Pamphlets in Your Language

We have multilingual information about your legal rights, available for free.

Law Reform & Test Cases

We work on important cases that can change laws and protect people in our community.

Community Organizing

We help community groups and tenant organizations get a voice in decisions that affect them.

Helpful Links & Resources

Here are some helpful resources for people looking for legal information and community services. Most of these organizations are partners in helping low-income residents in East Toronto access the support they need.

Legal Aid Ontario

Legal Aid Ontario (LAO) funds community legal clinics like ours across the province. LAO helps people who cannot afford a lawyer.

  • Legal Aid Ontario

    Learn more about legal aid in Ontario, find other legal clinics, or apply for legal aid.

    Visit Legal Aid Ontario →

Government Benefits & Services

Information and applications for government support programs.

  • Service Ontario

    Apply for Ontario Works, ODSP, and other provincial programs.

    Visit Service Ontario →

  • Service Canada

    Information about Employment Insurance (EI), Canada Pension Plan (CPP), and federal benefits.

    Visit Service Canada →

Housing & Tenant Rights

Resources for renters and information about your rights as a tenant.

  • Landlord and Tenant Board

    Ontario's official tribunal for landlord and tenant disputes. Information about your rights and how to file a case.

    Visit the Landlord and Tenant Board →

  • Toronto Tenants Union

    A membership-based organization that fights for tenant rights and organizes renters in Toronto.

    Visit Toronto Tenants Union →

Community Support & Services

Organizations offering support with food, housing, mental health, and other needs.

  • Woodgreen Community Centre

    Community programs, food support, housing support, and more for residents of East Toronto.

    Visit Woodgreen →

  • 211 Ontario

    A free service that connects you to social, health, and government services in your area. Call 2-1-1 or visit online.

    Visit 211 Ontario →

Family Violence & Safety

If you or someone you know is experiencing family violence, there is help available.

  • Assaulted Women's Helpline

    Free, confidential support 24/7 for women experiencing violence. Language interpretation available.

    Call: 1-866-863-0511

  • Family Services Toronto

    Counselling, support groups, and services for families experiencing violence and conflict.

    Visit Family Services Toronto →

Immigration & Settlement

Resources for newcomers and people navigating Canadian immigration.

  • Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)

    Official government information about immigration, permanent residence, and citizenship.

    Visit IRCC →

  • Woodgreen Community Centre Settlement Services

    Support for newcomers including orientation, language services, and employment help.

    Visit Woodgreen →

Employment & Workplace Rights

Information about your rights as a worker and employment services.

  • Ministry of Labour

    Ontario government information about workplace rights, minimum wage, and workplace standards.

    Visit Ministry of Labour →

  • Canadian Labour Program

    Federal workplace rights and standards information.

    Visit Canadian Labour Program →

Consumer Rights & Fraud

Help if you've been scammed or have a dispute with a merchant.

  • Consumer Protection Ontario

    Information about your consumer rights and how to file a complaint.

    Visit Consumer Protection Ontario →

  • Competition Bureau (Canada)

    Report fraud, scams, or false advertising to federal authorities.

    Visit Competition Bureau →

Legal Information & Self-Help

General legal information and self-help resources.

  • LawHelp Ontario

    Plain-language legal information and guides on many areas of law.

    Visit LawHelp Ontario →

  • Ontario Courthouse Libraries

    Free legal research and self-help information at courthouse libraries across Ontario.

    Visit Courthouse Libraries →

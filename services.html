What We Can Help With

Ontario Works & ODSP

Questions about Ontario Works or Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP) benefits? We can advise you on your rights and help with appeals.

Landlord & Tenant Issues

Having problems with your landlord or rental home? We can advise you on your rights as a tenant, lease agreements, eviction, repairs, and more.

Immigration

Need help understanding Canadian immigration laws or your status? We can provide legal advice on immigration matters.

Employment Insurance & Employment Law

Trouble with Employment Insurance (EI)? Questions about your rights at work? We can advise you on employment law and EI benefits.

Canada Pension Plan (CPP)

Questions about your Canada Pension Plan benefits? We can provide legal advice on CPP matters.

Criminal Injuries Compensation

Advice only. If you have been injured by a crime, we can advise you on compensation options.

Family Violence

Experiencing family violence? We can advise you on restraining orders, protection orders, and family law rights.

Consumer Law

Been scammed or sold faulty goods? We can advise you on your consumer rights.

Small Claims Court

Advice only. Have a dispute involving money? We can advise you about Small Claims Court.

Certified True Copies & Affidavits

Need a document certified as a true copy? We offer this service in limited areas. Call 416-461-8102 for details.

Special Clinics

Chinese-Speaking Legal Clinic

Every Tuesday afternoon, 1:30 pm - 4:00 pm. No appointment needed.

Multilingual Support

We can arrange interpretation in many languages. Call 416-461-8102 to ask about your language.

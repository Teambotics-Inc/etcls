Become a Member

Membership is free. As a member, you help shape what we do and have a voice in our community.

To become a member, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Live or work in our catchment area (bounded by Don River, Victoria Park, Danforth, and Lake Ontario)
  • Support our mission and values

Our Mission

Our mission guides everything we do. Make sure you agree with it before joining:

East Toronto Community Legal Services Inc. is a community legal resource centre with a human face.

• We serve people of modest income in our catchment area on an individual case-by-case basis

• We serve the community generally by providing outreach, legal education, and community organizing to agencies, individuals, and other groups in our community

• We balance providing legal help to individuals with taking on test cases and important law reform work

• We are responsive to the diverse cultural communities in our catchment area

• We network with other agencies in the community to help clients access other services they need

• We work to identify and understand the legal needs of our community

How to Apply

To become a member, please:

  1. Fill out a membership application form. You can pick one up at our office or contact us to request one by phone or email.
  2. Return the form to us. Mail it, bring it in person, or ask about other ways to submit it.
  3. You're a member! We'll confirm your membership and add you to our mailing list.

Questions? Call us at 416-461-8102 and we'll help you through the process.

Become a Board Member

Board members make important decisions about how we serve the community. We elect new board members each year from our membership.

What Board Members Do

Board members:

  • Attend monthly meetings (1–2 hours each)
  • Help oversee the clinic's work and finances
  • Work on committees on topics like finance, human resources, or law reform
  • Represent the community's voice in our decisions

We need board members from all backgrounds. We especially welcome members with lived experience of legal problems — many of the issues we help with, you may have faced yourself.

Who Can Be a Board Member?

You can be a board member if you are:

  • A member of East Toronto Community Legal Services
  • Willing to volunteer 5–6 hours each month
  • Interested in serving your community

How to Apply

If you are interested in becoming a board member, please contact us at 416-461-8102. We can tell you more about what's involved and answer your questions.

Why Become a Member?

  • Have a voice: Members help elect our board and help guide our priorities
  • Get involved: Serve on committees or volunteer with us
  • Stay informed: We keep members updated on our work and upcoming events
  • It's free: There are no membership fees
  • Support your community: Help a clinic that serves low-income people in East Toronto

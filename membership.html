Become a Member

Membership is free. As a member, you help shape what we do and have a voice in our community.

To become a member, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Live or work in our catchment area
  • Support our mission and values

Our Mission

Make sure you agree with our mission before joining:

East Toronto Community Legal Services Inc. is a community legal resource centre with a human face.

• We serve people of modest income on a case-by-case basis

• We serve the community generally by providing outreach, legal education, and community organizing

• We balance individual case work with test cases and law reform work

• We are responsive to diverse cultural communities in our catchment area

• We network with other agencies to help clients access services they need

• We work to identify and understand the legal needs of our community

How to Apply

  1. Fill out a membership application form
  2. Return the form to us (mail, in person, or other methods)
  3. You're a member!

Questions? Call us at 416-461-8102

Become a Board Member

Board members make important decisions about how we serve the community.

What Board Members Do

  • Attend monthly meetings
  • Help oversee the clinic's work and finances
  • Work on committees
  • Represent the community's voice

Who Can Apply

  • Be a member of ETCLS
  • Willing to volunteer 5-6 hours each month
  • Interested in serving your community

Interested? Call 416-461-8102
